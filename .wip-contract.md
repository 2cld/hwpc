## HWPC Goals (what success looks like)

1. **Mark runs routes independently** - phone app, printed tickets, minimal Chris involvement
2. **Accounting stays current** - QBOL sync monthly, tax filing on time
3. **hwpc-rp data is protected** - automated rsync backup to CyberTruck sync folder (daily)
4. **Business grows** - Google presence, Candy-bot, customer communication
5. **Chris spends less time on HWPC ops** - systems handle the routine

## Split-Brain Triggers (what falls through cracks)

| Risk | Symptom | Prevention |
|------|---------|------------|
| QBOL sync forgotten | Month passes with no export | @date tickler: first of month "QBOL sync pending" |
| Mark field issues unreported | Route problems accumulate | Weekly sync check "any Mark issues?" |
| Tax deadline missed | Filing date approaches silently | Existing tickler: IA 1065 Sep 30, 1040 Oct 15 |
| hwpc-rp database lost | cat9fin dies, rsync not running | Daily check: sync folder freshness on CyberTruck |
| Google/customer issues ignored | Reviews pile up | Monthly tickler: check Google Business profile |

Wip may:

- Create/update issues on ns-account (gitea)
