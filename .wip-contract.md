## Disaster Recovery Service Contract

**Proposed client contract (2026-11-03): documentation only. Provider-level
reporting is not deployed or recovery-certified by this change. Existing backup
and monitoring arrangements continue until their replacements are verified.**
The standard extension is proposed in
[2cld/netstack#39](https://github.com/2cld/netstack/pull/39).

HWPC consumes hosting and disaster recovery from **cf**, following the
[netstack service-data backup and restore standard](https://github.com/2cld/netstack/blob/master/docs/ops/backup/service-data-backup-restore-pattern.md).
HWPC specifies the service outcome; cf owns the backup machinery and any
federation storage arrangements. Wip coordinates, monitors and escalates.

| Term | HWPC requirement |
|------|------------------|
| Service ID | `hwpc-rp` (Route Print) |
| Provider | cf, per its [service/DR contract](https://github.com/2cld/cf/blob/main/.wip-contract.md) |
| Required data | Route Print operational data and the configuration needed to recover it; exact coverage inventory requires owner approval |
| RPO / RTO | Pending explicit agreement between HWPC and cf; no recovery SLA is established by this draft |
| Provider status | CF's `hwpc-rp` service-level protection result: captured-data freshness, coverage, recovery-test result/time and any unmet terms |
| Coordination status | Wip's independently timestamped assessment of that same service; agreed lookup/check mapping is pending implementation |
| Status locations | CF at `https://cf.cat9.me/status.json`; Wip at `https://wip.cat9.me/status.json`. Their current overall health is not yet a per-service DR result |
| Escalation | Wip routes missing, stale, failed or conflicting evidence through this contract's existing contact method |
| Adoption proof | Approved scope/terms and a successful isolated restore recorded in [HWPC's recovery drill](https://github.com/2cld/hwpc/issues/27), with CF's operator maintaining the recovery procedure |

HWPC must not infer protection from CF/Wip being reachable or reporting overall
`ok`. Its service-specific report remains pending until coverage, agreed terms,
and restore evidence exist. CF owns the receiver relationship and must keep
implementation details out of the client-facing result. HWPC need not know which
offsite site, path, transport or backup tool cf uses.

This contract does not move HWPC production, change its current backup route,
authorize customer-data access by Wip, or replace existing probes immediately.
The former 48-hour backup-age alert is a legacy threshold, not an agreed RPO.

## Split-Brain Triggers (what falls through cracks)

| Risk | Symptom | Prevention |
|------|---------|------------|
| QBOL sync forgotten | Month passes with no export | @date tickler: first of month "QBOL sync pending" |
| Mark field issues unreported | Route problems accumulate | Weekly sync check "any Mark issues?" |
| Tax deadline missed | Filing date approaches silently | Existing tickler: IA 1065 Sep 30, 1040 Oct 15 |
| hwpc-rp database lost | Service data cannot be recovered within agreed terms | Check cf's service-level protection and Wip's assessment; require recorded restore proof |
| Google/customer issues ignored | Reviews pile up | Monthly tickler: check Google Business profile |
